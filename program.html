Why the off-season

You can't rebuild a swing in the middle of a season. There's a game on Saturday, so every lesson turns into a patch job — get through this weekend, fix it properly later. Later never comes.

A clear month is different. Nothing to protect, nothing to undo on the weekend. We can pull something apart, let it feel awful for a week, and put it back together better. Three days a week is what makes that stick — the gap between sessions is short enough that the last one hasn't worn off.

Who it's for

A 14u and older player who's serious about playing after high school and has a genuine gap coming — after travel ball winds down, over winter, a school break. Somewhere they can give this month their full attention.

Not while they're in season. If your player has games every weekend and team practice midweek, this is too much on top and you won't get the benefit. Do the membership to tick over, and come back to this when their calendar clears.

How it works

  1. You apply below. Tell me about your player and — this matters most — when they've actually got a clear month.
  2. I read every one myself. If it's a fit I'll call or text you within a couple of days. If the timing's wrong I'll say so and tell you when to come back, rather than take your money for a month that won't work.
  3. We lock your three days. Same three days and time each week — Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 6pm, say. I block all twelve off at once so they're yours. No booking each one.
  4. Then we work. Every session builds on the last, with a plan for the month. Not twelve separate lessons that happen to be near each other.
