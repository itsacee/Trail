Who it's for

This isn't a once-a-week lesson. It's for a 14u and older player who wants to be seen by college coaches and is ready to put in real reps — the same three days every week for a month, on top of whatever their team is doing.

If your player is younger, or you're not sure they'll make every session, the membership is the better fit and you can always step up later.

How it works

  1. You apply below. Tell me about your player — age, position, what team they're on, what they're chasing.
  2. I read every one myself. If it's a fit I'll call or text you within a couple of days to talk it through. If it isn't, I'll tell you that straight and point you somewhere that is.
  3. We lock your three days. You pick the same three days and time each week — Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 6pm, say — and I block all twelve sessions off at once. No booking each one.
  4. Then we work. Every session builds on the last. You'll get a plan for the month, not twelve separate lessons.
