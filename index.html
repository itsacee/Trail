FAQ

Quick Answers

How does the membership work?

$240 for one month — 4 one-hour lessons. It does not auto-renew; buy again when you want another month. You pick your first day at checkout, then book the other three on any days that work for you inside that month — one at a time, whenever you know your schedule. Sign in at Member login with the same email to book them, reschedule a day, and to see how many you have left and the date they expire. Unused lessons don't roll over.

What days and times can we book?

Weeknight evenings and weekend afternoons. Hours change with the season, so the booking page always shows what's actually open — pick a day and the free times appear.

What ages do you train?

Age 7 through high school. Lessons are built around your player's age and skill — not a one-size program.

Where are lessons held?

Mustang High School baseball field. The address and directions come in your confirmation email. Questions? Call or text (405) 819-4401.

What should my player bring?

Bat, glove, turfs, and a water bottle.

What's your cancellation policy?

Members: sign in at Member login and tap Reschedule or Cancel at least 12 hours before the lesson. That credit stays on the membership so they can pick another day. Less than 12 hours notice may be charged a fee.

Drop-in (single or 30-min): those can't be changed online. Text or call (405) 819-4401 at least 12 hours ahead.
